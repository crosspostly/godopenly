A prayer while you are waiting for an answer

Most of us pray about waiting only when it gets bad enough to keep us awake. This page is for the smaller, daily version of that.

Waiting is the least photogenic part of faith. Nothing happens, nothing can be posted, and the delay starts to feel like an answer. Isaiah 40 is written for exactly that week.

Isaiah 40:31

But they that wait upon the Lord shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles; they shall run, and not be weary; and they shall walk, and not faint.

Psalm 27:14

Wait on the Lord: be of good courage, and he shall strengthen thine heart: wait, I say, on the Lord.

Lamentations 3:25

The Lord is good unto them that wait for him, to the soul that seeketh him.

There is nothing in the verse that asks you to feel better first. Say it while you still feel the same.

If you only have one minute

Read Isaiah 40:31 once. Then Pick a fixed time each morning this week. One psalm, one sentence of prayer.

How long should I keep praying for the same thing?

Until the answer comes or the desire changes. Psalm 27:14 repeats the instruction because it is easy to drop.
