A prayer before sleep when your head will not switch off

You do not need a long prayer for this. You need about four minutes and a verse that says it better than you can right now.

The last twenty minutes of the day decide the tone of the night. A phone at 23:40 is a room full of other people's opinions. Psalm 4:8 is what to say instead.

Psalm 4:8

I will both lay me down in peace, and sleep: for thou, Lord, only makest me dwell in safety.

Psalm 127:2

It is vain for you to rise up early, to sit up late, to eat the bread of sorrows: for so he giveth his beloved sleep.

Proverbs 3:24

When thou liest down, thou shalt not be afraid: yea, thou shalt lie down, and thy sleep shall be sweet.

Copy the verse into your notes app, or write it on the back of a receipt. Phones get scrolled; paper gets read.

If you only have one minute

Read Psalm 4:8 once. Then Phone face down across the room. Read Psalm 4:8 in bed, then close your eyes.

What if I wake up at 3 a.m. and start worrying?

Keep the verse by the bed. Read it before you reach for the phone; sleep often follows attention.
