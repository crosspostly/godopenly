A prayer for forgiveness when you keep going back to the same sin

If you are reading this at 23:40 with the phone in your hand, start here. One verse, one sentence, one small thing to do.

The same sin, the same Tuesday. Shame says the prayer has stopped working. John says the opposite: confession is the door you keep walking through.

1 John 1:9

If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.

Matthew 6:14

For if ye forgive men their trespasses, your heavenly Father will also forgive you:

Colossians 3:13

Forbearing one another, and forgiving one another, if any man have a quarrel against any: even as Christ forgave you, so also do ye.

Read it once to hear it. Read it a second time as if it were addressed to you, because it is.

If you only have one minute

Read 1 John 1:9 once. Then Read 1 John 1:9 out loud twice. The second time, believe it.

How many times can I ask for the same forgiveness?

Peter asked something similar and got a number he did not like. The limit is not the point; the Father is.
