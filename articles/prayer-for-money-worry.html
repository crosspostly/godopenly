A prayer when money is short this month

If you are reading this at 23:40 with the phone in your hand, start here. One verse, one sentence, one small thing to do.

Money worry has a sound: the calculator at midnight, the bank app you open four times a day. Jesus addresses it not with a budget but with birds and lilies.

Matthew 6:25-26

Therefore I say unto you, Take no thought for your life, what ye shall eat, or what ye shall drink; nor yet for your body, what ye shall put on. Is not the life more than meat, and the body than raiment? Behold the fowls of the air: for they sow not, neither do they reap, nor gather into barns; yet your heavenly Father feedeth them. Are ye not much better than they?

Philippians 4:19

But my God shall supply all your need according to his riches in glory by Christ Jesus.

Hebrews 13:5

Let your conversation be without covetousness; and be content with such things as ye have: for he hath said, I will never leave thee, nor forsake thee.

There is nothing in the verse that asks you to feel better first. Say it while you still feel the same.

If you only have one minute

Read Matthew 6:25-26 once. Then Write the number you are afraid of on paper, then write Matthew 6:26 underneath it.

Does trusting God mean I should not plan?

No. Proverbs rewards planning. The command in Matthew 6 is about worry, not spreadsheets.
