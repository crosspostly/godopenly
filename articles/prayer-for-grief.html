A prayer for grief when words will not come

You do not need a long prayer for this. You need about four minutes and a verse that says it better than you can right now.

Grief does not move in a line. You can be fine at the funeral and undone by a song in a shop three months later. Jesus wept at a tomb even knowing what He would do next.

Psalm 34:18

The Lord is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart; and saveth such as be of a contrite spirit.

Matthew 5:4

Blessed are they that mourn: for they shall be comforted.

Revelation of John 21:4

And God shall wipe away all tears from their eyes; and there shall be no more death, neither sorrow, nor crying, neither shall there be any more pain: for the former things are passed away.

There is nothing in the verse that asks you to feel better first. Say it while you still feel the same.

If you only have one minute

Read Psalm 34:18 once. Then You do not have to explain the loss to God. Read Psalm 34:18 and sit with it for a minute.

Is it okay to be angry at God while grieving?

The psalms do it constantly. Read Psalm 88 and notice how it ends: in the dark, with God still addressed.
