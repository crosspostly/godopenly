A prayer for a decision you keep postponing

If you are reading this at 23:40 with the phone in your hand, start here. One verse, one sentence, one small thing to do.

Most decisions are not dramatic. Two jobs, one move, a yes that would cost you every Saturday. James says the missing ingredient is usually wisdom, not information.

James 1:5

If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not; and it shall be given him.

Proverbs 3:5-6

Trust in the Lord with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding. In all thy ways acknowledge him, and he shall direct thy paths.

Psalm 32:8

I will instruct thee and teach thee in the way which thou shalt go: I will guide thee with mine eye.

Copy the verse into your notes app, or write it on the back of a receipt. Phones get scrolled; paper gets read.

If you only have one minute

Read James 1:5 once. Then Give the decision a date. Write it down, and pray James 1:5 every morning until then.

How do I know it is God and not my preference?

Test it in the ordinary ways: Scripture, counsel from someone who knows you, and whether it survives a week of prayer.
