A prayer for anger you are ashamed of

You do not need a long prayer for this. You need about four minutes and a verse that says it better than you can right now.

Anger is often the second feeling. Underneath it there is usually fear, or shame, or the sense that something was taken. James is blunt about what anger produces when it is left alone.

Ephesians 4:26

Be ye angry, and sin not: let not the sun go down upon your wrath:

James 1:19-20

Wherefore, my beloved brethren, let every man be swift to hear, slow to speak, slow to wrath: For the wrath of man worketh not the righteousness of God.

Proverbs 15:1

A soft answer turneth away wrath: but grievous words stir up anger.

There is nothing in the verse that asks you to feel better first. Say it while you still feel the same.

If you only have one minute

Read Ephesians 4:26 once. Then Before you answer the message that made you angry, wait ten minutes and pray James 1:19.

Should I wait before replying to an angry message?

Yes. James 1:19 puts hearing and slowness before speaking for a reason. Ten minutes is enough to change the reply.
