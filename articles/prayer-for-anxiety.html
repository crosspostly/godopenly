A prayer for anxiety when your mind will not stop

Most of us pray about anxiety only when it gets bad enough to keep us awake. This page is for the smaller, daily version of that.

Anxiety has a schedule. It shows up at 2 a.m., or in the queue at the supermarket, or the moment the house goes quiet. Paul wrote Philippians from a prison cell, which is why his answer is not 'calm down' but 'bring it, specifically'.

Philippians 4:6-7

Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God. And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.

1 Peter 5:7

Casting all your care upon him; for he careth for you.

Isaiah 41:10

Fear thou not; for I am with thee: be not dismayed; for I am thy God: I will strengthen thee; yea, I will help thee; yea, I will uphold thee with the right hand of my righteousness.

Read it once to hear it. Read it a second time as if it were addressed to you, because it is.

If you only have one minute

Read Philippians 4:6-7 once. Then Name the thing you are afraid of out loud, then read Philippians 4:6-7 once more, slowly.

What if I can't feel calm after praying?

Feelings arrive late. Say the verse anyway; the peace in Philippians 4:7 is described as a guard, not a mood.
